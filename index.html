Privacy Policy – GanitPro

Effective date: 3 November 2026
App: GanitPro – All in One Calculator
Developer / publisher: Zenu Studio
Privacy contact: zainab951612@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how GanitPro handles information when you use the Android application. GanitPro provides calculators for finance, tax, mathematics, business, property and daily-life use.

1. Summary

  • Calculator inputs and results are processed on your device by the app.
  • The app does not require you to create an account to use its calculator features.
  • The app displays advertisements using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) is used to present and manage applicable advertising privacy choices.
  • Advertising and consent SDKs may process device and advertising-related information as described below and in Google's policies.

2. Information processed by GanitPro

Calculator information. Values you enter into calculators (for example, amounts, rates, dates or other calculation inputs) are used locally by the app to calculate and display results. The app's calculator feature is designed to process these inputs on your device; the developer does not receive those calculator inputs through the calculator functionality. Please do not enter information that you do not want displayed on your device.

App preferences and recent activity. The app may store app preferences and convenience data locally on your device, such as favourites, recently used calculators, or settings, where those features are enabled. This information is used to provide app features and is not sent to the developer by the calculator functionality.

Advertising and consent information. GanitPro uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable consent and privacy choices. Depending on your device, region, consent choices and SDK configuration, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • IP address and approximate network-derived location;
  • device information, including device model, operating system and language;
  • advertising identifiers or other app/device identifiers;
  • ad views, interactions, diagnostics and fraud-prevention signals;
  • consent and privacy-choice signals.

This information may be used to deliver, measure, limit, personalize or provide non-personalized advertising (as applicable), prevent fraud, and comply with legal requirements. Exact processing depends on Google's services, your settings and applicable law.

For more information, review Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and Google Mobile Ads.

3. How information is used

  • perform calculator functions and show results;
  • remember locally stored app preferences and convenience features;
  • display and measure advertisements;
  • manage advertising consent and privacy choices;
  • maintain app security, prevent fraud and troubleshoot SDK or advertising issues;
  • comply with legal obligations.

The developer does not use calculator inputs for advertising.

4. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not sell calculator inputs. Calculator inputs are processed locally by the app's calculator functionality.

Advertising and consent functionality is provided by Google Mobile Ads and Google UMP. Information processed by these SDKs may be transmitted to Google and, where applicable, advertising or measurement partners for the purposes described in Section 2. Their handling of information is governed by their own terms and privacy policies. The developer does not control the independent data practices of Google or its partners.

5. Data storage, security, retention and deletion

  • Calculator inputs are processed locally by the app.
  • Local preferences and convenience data may remain on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Exact Android backup behaviour depends on the app/device backup settings.
  • Advertising-related information is handled and retained by Google under Google's applicable policies and settings.
  • The developer does not operate an account system for the calculator features described in this policy, so there is no GanitPro calculator account to delete.
  • You can clear locally stored app data through Android Settings, or uninstall the app. This does not delete information already processed by Google; consult Google for applicable privacy controls or deletion requests.

The developer takes reasonable steps to protect information under the developer's control. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Children's privacy

GanitPro is not designed specifically for children under the age defined by applicable law. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through an account system. Advertising SDKs may have their own age-related settings and requirements. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information should contact the developer using the contact details below.

Before release, the developer must ensure the app's target-audience declaration and AdMob/UMP age and consent configuration accurately match the app's intended audience and applicable law.
